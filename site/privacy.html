Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

OutOfBody ("the app") is published by CielPM, Inc. This policy explains what the app collects and why.

Your quit data stays on your phone

Your answers, plan, logged hits, cravings, check-ins, reasons and signature are stored on your device only. There is no account and we do not upload this content to our servers. Deleting the app, or using "Erase all data" in the app, removes it.

Usage analytics

To understand which screens people use and where they drop off, the app sends anonymous usage events (for example "onboarding step viewed", "hit logged", "craving beaten") with a random identifier, app version and device type to PostHog. These events do not include your name, reasons, notes or signature.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use Superwall to show paywalls and to receive subscription status from Apple (trial started, renewed, canceled). We never see your payment details.

Advertising measurement

If you allow tracking when the app asks, the Meta (Facebook) SDK records app installs, trial starts and purchases so we can measure which ads work. If you decline, the SDK only sends aggregated, non-identifying measurement as permitted by Apple. You can change this any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Health information

The app is a habit tool, not a medical service. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your quit data for advertising.

Children

The app is intended for adults 18+.

Your choices

You can turn off notifications and tracking in iOS Settings, and erase all app data from the You tab. For questions or data requests, contact hi@cielpm.ai.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes.
